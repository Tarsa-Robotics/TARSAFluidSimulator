TARSA — Design System01 · The idea
What TARSA is

A coordinate held in tension.

Four motorized reels anchor at the facade corners — two on the roof, two in the ground — and tension a small payload (a camera and a rotating nozzle) anywhere across the facade plane. It cleans the way facade drones do: soap and deionized water, pumped from the ground through the tether and applied under pressure. The difference is that TARSA is tethered, not flown — no battery aloft, no flying mass over the street, no airspace regulation, and no height ceiling.

Feel

Precision. Beauty. Trust. The system reads as an engineering datum — a drafting table, not a sales pitch.

Audience

Property managers, CFOs, facility engineers. Signals credibility and rigor — never venture hype.

Reference

Dieter Rams' Ten Principles govern every decision. If an element only decorates, it is removed.

TARSA — Design System02 · Logo
The mark

Four corners, one payload.

The mark is the machine, abstracted to its honest geometry: four corner reels, four cables, one small payload held at the center. The payload renders as a neutral square — its real orientation is still TBD, so the mark commits to no rotation. The wordmark is Archivo, no custom letterforms.

Clearspace & minimum size

Keep clearspace equal to the payload square on all sides. Minimum mark size 16px / 5mm; below that, drop the descriptor line.

Don't

No rotation, no recoloring the cables, no gradient, no outline-only mark, no setting the wordmark in another typeface.

TARSA — Design System03 · Color
Color

Cool, mineral, quiet — one signal.

Graphite ink and cool mineral neutrals on a drafting-paper ground. Color is an event, not a wash: a single vermilion signal marks the live, measured, or most-important thing, and never covers more than ~2% of a surface.

TARSA — Design System04 · Typography
Typography

Archivo + IBM Plex Mono.

Archivo — an engineered grotesque — carries display and body. IBM Plex Mono is the instrument voice: eyebrows, spec keys, captions, and all data. Two families, no third. Headings set tight and heavy; mono labels tracked and uppercase.

TARSA — Design System05 · Space & components
Space, shape & components

Sharp, hairline, exact.

An 8px base grid, near-square corners (0/2/4/8), and depth from hairlines before shadows. Components are quiet: ink primary, signal reserved for the single most important action.

TARSA — Design System06 · Principles
The test every decision passes

Less, but better.

Dieter Rams' Ten Principles are the brief. The kill list below always fails it.

01Innovative — improve meaningfully on prior form
06Honest — claims only what it delivers
02Useful — every feature earns its place
07Long-lasting — avoids trends, ages well
03Aesthetic — beauty from precision, not ornament
08Thorough — nothing left to chance
04Understandable — explains itself, no manual
09Environmentally friendly — conserves, no excess
05Unobtrusive — a tool, not a statement
10As little as possible — strip to essentials
Kill list — always fails

Side-stripe accent borders · gradient text · default glassmorphism · the hero-metric template · identical icon-card grids · modal-first thinking · warm photo grades & fake grain · emoji · rounded "friendly" everything · bluish-purple gradients.
